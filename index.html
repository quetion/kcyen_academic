關於我

目前任職於東吳大學經濟學系，並兼任國立臺灣大學財務金融學系教授。過去曾任國立臺灣大學財務金融學系博士後研究員，並於 2018 年取得臺灣大學財務金融博士學位，指導教授為王耀輝教授。

我的研究聚焦於加密貨幣金融、期貨與選擇權的實證議題。研究成果曾發表於 European Financial Management、Journal of Futures Markets、Finance Research Letters、Quarterly Review of Economics and Finance 等期刊。

I am a Professor in the Department of Economics at Soochow University and an Adjunct Professor in the Department of Finance at National Taiwan University. I received my Ph.D. in Finance from National Taiwan University in 2018 under the supervision of Professor Yaw-Huei Wang.

My research focuses on empirical questions in cryptocurrency finance, futures, and options. My work has appeared in European Financial Management, Journal of Futures Markets, Finance Research Letters, and Quarterly Review of Economics and Finance.
